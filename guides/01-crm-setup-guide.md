# ClientEngine CRM Setup Guide

## Your Complete Guide to Setting Up Your CRM for Maximum Results

---

## What This Guide Covers

This guide walks you through setting up your CRM (Customer Relationship Management) system from scratch — or fixing one that's not working. By the end, you'll have a clean, organized system that captures leads, tracks conversations, and helps you close more deals.

---

## Part 1: Before You Start

### What You Need
- Access to your CRM account (GoHighLevel/LeadProspector or similar)
- List of your current lead sources
- Rough idea of your sales process
- 2-3 hours of focused time

### What We'll Build
- Clean contact organization
- Lead capture forms
- Pipeline stages that match your sales process
- Tags and segments for easy filtering
- Basic automation to save you time

## Part 2: Setting Up Your Contact Structure

### Step 1: Clean Up Your Contact Fields

**Why this matters:** Messy data = lost leads. When every contact has the same fields filled out, you can filter, sort, and follow up properly.

**What to do:**

1. Go to Settings → Custom Fields
2. Review the default fields (name, email, phone)
3. Add these essential custom fields:
   - Lead Source (where they came from)
   - Business Type (if B2B)
   - Lead Temperature (Hot/Warm/Cold)
   - Next Action Date
   - Notes/Context

**Pro tip:** Don't add 50 fields. Start with 5-7 that you'll actually use.

---

### Step 2: Create Your Lead Sources

**Why this matters:** You need to know which marketing is working. If you don't track sources, you're flying blind.

**Common lead sources to set up:**
- Website form
- Referral
- Social media (specify which platform)
- Event/Trade show
- Cold outreach
- Paid ads
- Partner referral

**How to set it up:**
1. Create a dropdown field called "Lead Source"
2. Add your sources as options
3. Make it required on all forms

## Part 3: Building Your Pipeline

### Step 3: Map Your Sales Process

**Why this matters:** Your pipeline should match how you actually sell. Not some textbook process — YOUR process.

**Ask yourself:**
1. What happens first when someone shows interest?
2. What do you do to qualify them?
3. When do you present your offer?
4. What does the close look like?
5. What happens after they buy?

**Example pipeline for a service business:**
1. New Lead (captured but not contacted)
2. Contacted (initial outreach done)
3. Qualified (fit confirmed, need established)
4. Proposal Sent (offer delivered)
5. Negotiation (discussing terms)
6. Closed Won (deal done)
7. Closed Lost (not moving forward)

---

### Step 4: Build Your Pipeline Stages

**How to set it up:**

1. Go to Opportunities → Pipelines
2. Click "Create Pipeline" or edit existing
3. Add each stage from your mapped process
4. Set a probability % for each stage (your best guess at close rate)

**Stage setup tips:**
- Keep it to 5-7 stages max
- Name stages as actions ("Proposal Sent" not "Proposal")
- Make it obvious when a contact moves forward

## Part 4: Tagging and Segmentation

### Step 5: Create Your Tag System

**Why this matters:** Tags let you filter contacts instantly. Want to email everyone who attended last month's webinar? Tags make that possible.

**Three types of tags you need:**

**1. Status Tags (where they are)**
- Customer
- Prospect
- Former Customer
- Partner

**2. Interest Tags (what they want)**
- Interested in [Service A]
- Interested in [Service B]
- Price Sensitive
- Ready to Buy

**3. Action Tags (what to do)**
- Follow Up This Week
- Send Proposal
- Needs Nurturing
- Hot Lead

**How to create tags:**
1. Go to Contacts → Tags
2. Click "Create Tag"
3. Use clear, consistent naming (no spaces, use hyphens)
4. Create 10-15 core tags to start

---

### Step 6: Build Smart Lists

**Why this matters:** Smart lists update automatically. Set them up once, they work forever.

**Smart lists to create:**

1. **Hot Leads** (tag = "Hot Lead" AND last activity < 7 days)
2. **Follow Up Needed** (next action date = today or past)
3. **New This Week** (created date = this week)
4. **Customers** (tag = "Customer")
5. **Cold Leads** (no activity > 30 days)

**How to build:**
1. Go to Contacts → Smart Lists
2. Click "Create Smart List"
3. Add filters based on tags, dates, or fields
4. Save and name it clearly

## Part 5: Lead Capture Setup

### Step 7: Create Your First Capture Form

**Why this matters:** This is how leads get INTO your system. A bad form = lost leads.

**Every form needs:**
- Name field (required)
- Email field (required)
- Phone field (optional but recommended)
- One qualifying question
- Clear submit button

**How to build:**
1. Go to Sites → Forms
2. Click "Create Form"
3. Add your fields
4. Set the "Lead Source" field to auto-fill
5. Connect it to a pipeline stage (usually "New Lead")
6. Add a tag on submission

---

### Step 8: Set Up Multiple Capture Points

**Different forms for different situations:**

| Form Type | Purpose | Key Question |
|-----------|---------|--------------|
| Contact Us | General inquiry | "What's your biggest challenge with [topic]?" |
| Free Consultation | Book a call | "What would success look like for you?" |
| Download Lead Magnet | Get resource | "What's your current monthly revenue?" |
| Event Registration | Webinar/workshop | "What's your #1 goal this year?" |

**Pro tip:** Each form should tag leads differently so you know their intent.

## Part 6: Basic Automation

### Step 9: Welcome Sequence

**Why this matters:** Most leads go cold because no one follows up. Automation fixes that.

**Simple 3-email welcome sequence:**

**Email 1 (Immediate):** Welcome + what to expect
- Subject: "You're in — here's what's next"
- Content: Confirm what they signed up for, set expectations

**Email 2 (Day 2):** Value + proof
- Subject: "How [similar person] solved [problem]"
- Content: Case study or success story

**Email 3 (Day 5):** Soft CTA
- Subject: "Quick question about [their situation]"
- Content: Ask a question, invite a reply or call

**How to set up:**
1. Go to Automation → Workflows
2. Create new workflow
3. Trigger: Form submitted
4. Add email steps with delays
5. Activate

---

### Step 10: Pipeline Automation

**Automate the obvious stuff:**

| When This Happens | Do This |
|-------------------|---------|
| Lead moves to "Proposal Sent" | Send follow-up email in 3 days if no response |
| Lead marked "Closed Lost" | Send "What happened?" survey in 7 days |
| New lead created | Assign to sales rep + send Slack notification |
| Lead inactive 14 days | Send re-engagement email |

**Start small:** Pick 2-3 automations that will save you the most time.

## Part 7: Daily CRM Habits

### The 10-Minute Daily Routine

**Morning (5 minutes):**
1. Check "Follow Up Needed" smart list
2. Update any stale opportunities
3. Add notes from yesterday's calls

**Evening (5 minutes):**
1. Log today's activities
2. Set tomorrow's priorities
3. Clear your "New Leads" list

---

## Part 8: Weekly CRM Review

**Every Friday, spend 30 minutes:**

1. **Clean up data** — merge duplicates, update old records
2. **Review pipeline** — move stalled deals forward or mark lost
3. **Check metrics** — new leads, conversion rates, revenue
4. **Plan next week** — who needs follow-up, what needs attention
