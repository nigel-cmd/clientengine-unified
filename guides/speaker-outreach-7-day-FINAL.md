## Usage Notes

### Personalization Fields
- `[Name]` — Prospect's first name
- `[Event Name]` — Name of the event where you met
- `[Your name]` — Your name/signature

### Send Logic
- Days 0-4: Send to all prospects
- Days 5-6: Only send to engaged prospects (opened/clicked)

### CTA Links
- Replace `[Book a call here]` with your Calendly/GoHighLevel booking link
- Replace `[Watch the video]` with your Loom/YouTube video link
- Replace `[Book your free Digital Audit]` with audit booking link

### Tone Notes
- Keep it human and conversational
- No corporate speak
- One emotional driver per email (don't mix Speed + Money + Relief)
- PAS structure: Always state problem, agitate the pain, then solve
