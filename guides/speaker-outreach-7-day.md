## Usage Notes

### Personalization Fields to Update:
- [First Name] — Prospect's first name
- [Event Name] — Name of the event where you met
- [Topic] — Topic of their session/talk
- [Your Name] — Your name

### Timing:
- Day 0: Send immediately after meeting
- Day 1: 24 hours later if no response
- Day 2: 48 hours after Day 0 if no response
- Day 3: 72 hours after Day 0 if no response
- Day 4: 96 hours after Day 0 if no response (break-up email)
- Day 5: Only if they replied "tell me more" to Day 4
- Day 6: Only if they engaged with Day 5

### CTA Links:
Replace `[Book a 15-minute call here]`, `[Book a quick call]`, etc. with your actual Calendly or GoHighLevel booking link.

### Tone Reminders:
- Keep it human and conversational
- No jargon or hype words
- Focus on their pain (lost leads) not our features
- Every email provides some value, even if they don't buy
