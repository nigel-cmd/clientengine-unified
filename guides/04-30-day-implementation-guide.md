# ClientEngine 30-Day Implementation Guide

## From Zero to Fully-Systemized in 30 Days

---

## What This Guide Covers

This is your day-by-day roadmap to implementing ClientEngine and transforming your follow-up system. No overwhelm, no confusion — just clear daily actions that build on each other. By Day 30, you'll have a working system that captures leads, books appointments, and moves prospects toward a sale automatically.

---

## Before You Start

### What You Need
- Access to your CRM (GoHighLevel/LeadProspector)
- 30-60 minutes per day for implementation
- Your current lead sources identified
- A clear offer (what you sell and for how much)

### What You'll Have by Day 30
- Clean, organized CRM
- Lead capture forms on your website
- Automated follow-up sequences
- Working appointment booking system
- Performance dashboard
- 30 days of content/emails written
- Trained habits for daily CRM use

---

## WEEK 1: Foundation (Days 1-7)

**Theme:** Get your house in order. Clean data, clear structure.

---

### Day 1: Audit Your Current State

**Time:** 45 minutes

**Actions:**
1. Export all contacts from your current system
2. Count how many leads you have
3. Identify where leads currently come from
4. List the tools you're using (spreadsheets, email, etc.)
5. Document your current follow-up process (or lack thereof)

**Deliverable:** One-page audit document

**Questions to answer:**
- How many leads did we get last month?
- What percentage became customers?
- Where do leads get lost?
- What's working? What's not?

---

### Day 2: Set Up Your CRM Structure

**Time:** 60 minutes

**Actions:**
1. Log into your CRM
2. Review and customize contact fields
3. Create custom fields for your business:
   - Lead Source
   - Lead Temperature
   - Next Action Date
   - Notes
4. Set up your lead source dropdown

**Deliverable:** CRM structure ready for data

**Reference:** CRM Setup Guide (Deliverable #1)

---

### Day 3: Import and Clean Your Contacts

**Time:** 60 minutes

**Actions:**
1. Import your contact list
2. Merge duplicates
3. Fill in missing lead sources (best guess is fine)
4. Tag existing customers
5. Tag hot prospects

**Deliverable:** Clean contact database

**Pro tip:** Don't aim for perfect. Aim for usable. You can clean more later.

---

### Day 4: Map Your Sales Process

**Time:** 45 minutes

**Actions:**
1. Write down your current sales steps
2. Identify where leads typically get stuck
3. Define what moves someone to the next stage
4. Draw your pipeline on paper

**Deliverable:** Sales process map

**Example stages:**
1. New Lead
2. Contacted
3. Qualified
4. Proposal Sent
5. Negotiation
6. Closed Won/Lost

---

### Day 5: Build Your Pipeline

**Time:** 45 minutes

**Actions:**
1. Create your pipeline in CRM
2. Add stages from your map
3. Set probability percentages
4. Move existing opportunities to correct stages

**Deliverable:** Working pipeline

---

### Day 6: Create Your Tag System

**Time:** 30 minutes

**Actions:**
1. Create 10-15 core tags:
   - Customer
   - Prospect
   - Hot Lead
   - Cold Lead
   - Referral Source
   - [Your service] Interest
2. Apply tags to existing contacts
3. Set up 3-5 smart lists

**Deliverable:** Tag system + smart lists

---

### Day 7: Week 1 Review

**Time:** 30 minutes

**Actions:**
1. Review what you built this week
2. Test your setup (create a test contact)
3. Note any issues to fix
4. Plan Week 2

**Deliverable:** Week 1 completion checklist

**Weekend Homework:**
- Think about your ideal customer's biggest problem
- Draft 3 email subject lines that would grab their attention

---

## WEEK 2: Capture and Attract (Days 8-14)

**Theme:** Get leads flowing into your system automatically.

---

### Day 8: Create Your Lead Magnet

**Time:** 60 minutes

**Actions:**
1. Choose a lead magnet type:
   - Checklist
   - Template
   - Short guide
   - Video training
   - Tool/calculator
2. Outline the content
3. Create the asset (or hire it out)

**Deliverable:** Lead magnet ready to give away

**Quick win:** Repurpose something you already have.

---

### Day 9: Build Your First Capture Form

**Time:** 45 minutes

**Actions:**
1. Create form in your CRM
2. Add fields: Name, Email, Phone
3. Add one qualifying question
4. Set lead source automatically
5. Connect to "New Lead" pipeline stage

**Deliverable:** Working capture form

---

### Day 10: Write Your Welcome Email

**Time:** 45 minutes

**Actions:**
1. Write immediate confirmation email
2. Include lead magnet delivery
3. Set expectations for what's next
4. Add your contact info

**Deliverable:** Welcome email ready

**Template:**
```
Subject: Here's your [lead magnet]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for downloading [lead magnet]. Here's the link:
[LINK]

Over the next few days, I'll share some strategies that have helped [type of business] like yours [get result].

If you have questions, just reply to this email.

Talk soon,
[Your name]
```

---

### Day 11: Create Your 5-Day Nurture Sequence

**Time:** 90 minutes

**Actions:**
Write 5 emails:
- Day 0: Welcome + lead magnet
- Day 1: Story/problem agitation
- Day 2: Solution overview
- Day 3: Case study/social proof
- Day 4: Soft pitch/CTA
- Day 5: Direct CTA

**Deliverable:** 5-email sequence written

**Reference:** Use your CRM Setup Guide for automation setup

---

### Day 12: Set Up Your Automation

**Time:** 60 minutes

**Actions:**
1. Build workflow in CRM
2. Set trigger (form submitted)
3. Add email steps with delays
4. Test with your email address
5. Activate

**Deliverable:** Automated nurture sequence live

---

### Day 13: Install Forms on Your Website

**Time:** 45 minutes

**Actions:**
1. Get embed code from CRM
2. Add to website:
   - Homepage
   - Contact page
   - Blog posts
3. Test each form
4. Set up thank you page

**Deliverable:** Forms live on website

---

### Day 14: Week 2 Review + Lead Source Setup

**Time:** 45 minutes

**Actions:**
1. Test complete lead flow
2. Create forms for other sources:
   - Landing page
   - Facebook
   - Referral page
3. Document your lead sources
4. Plan Week 3

**Deliverable:** Multiple capture points active

---

## WEEK 3: Convert and Book (Days 15-21)

**Theme:** Turn interest into booked appointments.

---

### Day 15: Set Up Your Booking Calendar

**Time:** 60 minutes

**Actions:**
1. Connect your calendar
2. Set working hours
3. Add buffer time
4. Create appointment types:
   - Discovery Call (15 min)
   - Strategy Session (30 min)

**Deliverable:** Booking system ready

**Reference:** Appointment Booking Setup Guide (Deliverable #2)

---

### Day 16: Customize Your Booking Page

**Time:** 45 minutes

**Actions:**
1. Add headline and subheadline
2. Upload your photo
3. Write "what to expect" section
4. Add qualifying questions
5. Customize confirmation email

**Deliverable:** Professional booking page

---

### Day 17: Connect Booking to Your CRM

**Time:** 45 minutes

**Actions:**
1. Set automation on new booking:
   - Tag as "Call Booked"
   - Move to "Scheduled Call" stage
   - Add to calendar
2. Create reminder sequence:
   - 24 hours before
   - 2 hours before
3. Set up no-show follow-up

**Deliverable:** Integrated booking system

---

### Day 18: Write Your Sales Script

**Time:** 60 minutes

**Actions:**
Create a simple script:
1. Rapport building (2 min)
2. Problem discovery (10 min)
3. Solution presentation (10 min)
4. Objection handling (5 min)
5. Close/next steps (3 min)

**Deliverable:** Sales call script

**Key questions to include:**
- "What's your biggest challenge with [topic]?"
- "What would solving this mean for your business?"
- "What have you tried before?"
- "If we could [solution], would that be worth [price]?"

---

### Day 19: Create Your Proposal Template

**Time:** 45 minutes

**Actions:**
1. Build proposal template
2. Include:
   - Recap of their problem
   - Your solution
   - Investment
   - Timeline
   - Next steps
3. Save as reusable template

**Deliverable:** Proposal template ready

---

### Day 20: Set Up Follow-Up Automation for Calls

**Time:** 45 minutes

**Actions:**
1. Create post-call sequences:
   - If interested: Follow-up sequence
   - If not ready: Nurture sequence
   - If no-show: Reschedule sequence
2. Add to CRM automation

**Deliverable:** Post-call follow-up system

---

### Day 21: Week 3 Review + Test Everything

**Time:** 45 minutes

**Actions:**
1. Test complete booking flow
2. Role-play a sales call
3. Review all automations
4. Fix any issues
5. Plan Week 4

**Deliverable:** Booking and sales system tested

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

**"I don't have time for this every day"**
- Batch similar tasks on weekends
- Start with just Week 1, expand later
- Hire help for technical setup

**"My data is too messy to clean"**
- Focus on last 6 months only
- Archive old contacts
- Good enough is better than perfect

**"I'm not a writer"**
- Use templates provided
- Record videos instead
- Hire a copywriter for key pieces

**"This feels overwhelming"**
- Do one week at a time
- Take breaks between weeks
- Remember: progress > perfection
